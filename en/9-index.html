FAQ

Can I start for free?

Yes. The free plan includes one store, 5 ready templates and up to 500 products. The platform commission is taken from sales.

How do I get paid?

Link a Vodafone Cash number or an InstaPay account, track every payment and withdraw earnings from the finance page.

Can I change how my store looks?

Yes. Pick a template and edit texts, images, colors and fonts from the site design page.

Is the platform in Arabic and English?

Yes. The platform is available in both Arabic and English, with Egyptian Arabic as the default.
