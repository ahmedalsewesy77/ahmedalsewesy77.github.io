Help center

Answers to common questions.

Is the free plan really free?

Yes. One store, 5 templates, 500 products and a 14% commission on sales. No card needed.

Do I need my own domain?

No. Your store gets a free link on the platform. A custom domain comes with Ultra.

How do shoppers pay?

Cash on delivery, InstaPay and Vodafone Cash. Card payment is simulated in this version.

Can I change templates later?

Yes, anytime. Your products and orders stay as they are.

How do I upgrade?

From the dashboard plan page. Payment is a demo in this version.

Is my data safe?

Data lives in a real database with row-level access rules; prices and stock are computed server-side.
